# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want one place for personal and professional connections. The product is intended to support both kinds of social interaction; more specific audience segments remain undecided.

## Product Purpose

Help people maintain connections by managing a profile and friends, sharing updates with chosen privacy, and responding to posts with reactions and comments.

## Positioning

A straightforward, general-purpose social network that brings personal and professional connections together. No distinctive product mechanism has been established.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The documented backend supports registration and login, current and public profiles, user search, friend lists and requests, privacy-filtered paginated feeds, post creation/editing/deletion, image and media attachments, reactions, reposts, and comments. Post privacy values are public, friends, and only me. The frontend should not imply capabilities beyond the backend API. Product name and branding are undecided.

## Evidence on Hand

The backend repository README documents the API routes and response format. The frontend repository contains an early Next.js implementation and API service modules. No confirmed logo, brand assets, customer evidence, or testimonials are established.

## Product Principles

- Support personal and professional connections without claiming to specialize in either.
- Make sharing privacy explicit and understandable.
- Keep social actions grounded in the backend's supported capabilities.
- Do not present invented users, activity, or proof as real.
