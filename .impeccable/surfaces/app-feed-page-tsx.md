# Feed surface

## Scope and mode

Authenticated social feed. Operate.

## Audience and task

Members balancing personal and professional connections use the feed to catch up with people and share updates.

## Primary action and content

The primary action is publishing a text or photo post. Feed entries come from the authenticated API and can be paginated, reacted to, commented on, and shared when public. The composer must show the audience setting before a post is published.

## Constraints

Use only capabilities and data returned by the backend. Do not fabricate posts, users, social proof, engagement counts, or online presence. Support the API privacy values: public, friends, and only me.

## Direction and memorable moment

Use familiar social-feed structure with a clear blue action, neutral reading surfaces, and a responsive profile / feed / connections frame. The memorable moment is the post composer pairing an audience choice with the share action.

The product name, brand identity, and any product-specific differentiator remain undecided.
